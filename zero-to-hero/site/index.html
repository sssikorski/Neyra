FAQ

Good questions.

Is it free?

Creating your Hero is free to start. To submit, pick a package from $6 per Hero.

Do I need to be an artist?

No. A rough sketch is enough. It proves the idea is yours.

Can I submit more than once?

Yes. Each Hero is its own submission with its own scene. More Heroes, more chances.

How are winners picked?

A jury scores every Hero and its scene on public criteria. 100 Heroes share the $10,000 pool.

Is the Hero mine?

Yes. You author and own your Hero. NEYRA makes no ownership claim, never reuses it for anyone else and never trains AI on it.

How do I pay?

UPI, cards, local wallets and PayPal, depending on your country.

Who runs this?

NEYRA, in partnership with Freelancer.com.
