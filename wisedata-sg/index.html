AI needs your knowledge.
Your knowledge needs a lock.

The more your AI can read, the more useful it is. That is also the risk. Most companies end up choosing between useful AI and private data.

Cloud AI reads what you send.

Files pasted into public AI tools leave your control, and often your country.

Knowledge is scattered.

Contracts in email, numbers in spreadsheets, decisions in chats. AI can't use what it can't find.

Data has to stay in-country.

Regulators and clients ask more and more where data sits and who can read it.

Sealed before it leaves.
Kept on your nodes.

How Ghostdrive protects a file, step by step.

  1. 01LIVE
    Sealed on the device

    Files are encrypted on the laptop or phone before they move. Keys come from a passkey or 24 recovery words and stay with you.

    AES-256-GCM · HKDF-SHA256
  2. 02LIVE
    Split into blocks

    Each file becomes small blocks, each checked against its fingerprint on read. Edit a page, and only the changed blocks sync.

    Blocks ≤256 KiB · SHA2-256
  3. 03LIVE
    Kept by your nodes

    Blocks live on your devices and on company nodes in your office or data centre. Ghostdrive runs no server that holds your data.

    Company node · backup node
  4. 04LIVE
    Read by your AI

    The model runs on the device or on your node. Wise Office works from your Data Room, and asks before it spends, signs or sends.

    On-device model · your node
