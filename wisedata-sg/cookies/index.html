1. In short

Our website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It does load web fonts from Google, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details when you visit.

This policy explains that in more detail. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. They are commonly used to remember settings, measure traffic and show advertising.

When you submit a contact form, your details are sent to our form delivery provider and then to us. That is described in our Privacy Policy; it does not involve cookies.

4. Your choices

Because we do not set cookies, there is nothing to switch off on our site. You can still control cookies and similar technologies in your browser settings, and you can use your browser's or an extension's tools to block requests to Google Fonts; the site will then show a standard system font instead.

5. If this changes

If we later add analytics or other technologies that use cookies or similar identifiers, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent before they are used.
